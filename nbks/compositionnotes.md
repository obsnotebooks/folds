## Basic idea
In my copy of _A Mirror of the Sea_ I noticed that the book’s running headers carried chapter titles over numbered shorter subsections of the text. This offers something like a paratextual L-cut in which a running header over, for example, the final paragraph of a section may read as applying to the beginning of a new numbered section on the same page.

_Folds_ will make use of paratext in a similar way that will bring together animated versions of my ongoing ‘philosophical’ poems.
- the screen layout will be configure like a facing-pages opening
- poems will be set continously across the opening, then ‘overleaf,’ with divisions between poems indicated by an ornament or by numbering
- poems will be animated making use of recorded-performance or `timeToRead()` timestamping, as developed from _Scoring the Spelt Air_ 
- poem titles will run as headings over both ‘pages’ of the opening, and will operate with the L-cut pattern.
